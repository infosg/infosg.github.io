# Identity & Documents

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Confirm all details on the official sites._

Your NRIC, passport and Singpass account are the basis for most government and bank transactions.

## NRIC (identity card)

- Singapore citizens are issued an NRIC at a young age and must re-register at set ages.
- Report a **lost or stolen NRIC** promptly to the Police and ICA, and apply for a replacement.
- Update your **registered address** with ICA when you move. Many agencies read your address from this record.
- Keep your NRIC number private. Only share it when there is a legitimate need.

## Passport

- Apply or renew through ICA. Check the **validity requirement** of the country you are travelling to (many require 6 months) well before you book.
- Report a lost or stolen passport at once, including while overseas, and contact the nearest Singapore embassy or consulate.

## Singpass

- Singpass is the national digital identity for government and many private services.
- Set up the **Singpass app** and turn on **face verification** or a PIN for login.
- Keep your account secure: never share your login or one-time codes, and only log in through the official Singpass app or website.
- You can reset or recover your account through the Singpass website.

## Official sources
