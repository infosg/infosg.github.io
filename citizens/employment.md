# Employment & Workplace Rights

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Confirm all details with MOM._

The Ministry of Manpower (MOM) sets and enforces most workplace rules.

## Your basic rights

- You should receive **key employment terms** in writing, covering salary, hours, leave and notice period.
- The **Employment Act** sets minimum standards for most employees, including rest days, public holidays, annual leave, sick leave and salary payment timing. Some categories of employees are covered differently, so check your coverage on MOM.
- Salary should be paid on time. Deductions are only allowed in set circumstances.
- Employers must make **CPF contributions** for citizens and PRs.

## Fair treatment

- The **Workplace Fairness** laws and the **Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices** prohibit discrimination on grounds such as age, race, religion, sex, disability and marital status.
- Harassment at work can be reported to the employer, TAFEP or MOM.

## Work injury and safety

- Employees injured at work, or who contract an occupational disease, may be entitled to **work injury compensation**. Report the injury to your employer as soon as possible.
- Unsafe workplaces can be reported to MOM.

## If you lose your job

- Employers must give notice or pay in lieu as set out in your contract or the Employment Act.
- Look into **SkillsFuture** schemes, career coaching and job-matching support through Workforce Singapore and the national jobs portal.
- Check your eligibility for **temporary financial support** (see [Support schemes](support-schemes.html)).

## Resolving disputes

- Start with your employer or HR.
- Salary and wrongful dismissal claims go through the **Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management (TADM)** before the Employment Claims Tribunals.
