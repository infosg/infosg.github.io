# Education & Skills

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Confirm all details with MOE and SkillsFuture._

## The school system in outline

- **Primary school:** Primary 1 to 6, ending with the **PSLE**. Primary 1 registration is by phases, with priority based on factors such as citizenship and proximity.
- **Secondary school:** courses and subject levels are chosen from PSLE results, with later options to move between courses.
- **Post-secondary:** junior colleges, the Millennia Institute, polytechnics and the Institute of Technical Education (ITE), as well as the Singapore universities and private institutions.
- Education is **compulsory** for citizens of primary school age, with limited exemptions.

## Help with costs

- Government and government-aided schools charge **subsidised fees**.
- **MOE Financial Assistance Scheme** and school-level bursaries help eligible lower-income families.
- Tertiary students can look at bursaries, tuition grants and loans.

## SkillsFuture for adults

- **SkillsFuture Credit** helps eligible citizens pay for approved courses. Amounts and top-ups vary, so check the official site.
- Mid-career and older workers may get additional training support.
- Browse approved courses and use the skills and career tools on the **MySkillsFuture** portal.

## Other tools

- **Parents Gateway** for school communications, consents and notices.
- School counsellors and the MOE hotline for concerns about special educational needs.
