# Support Schemes

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Eligibility and amounts change, often at the annual Budget. Confirm on the official sites._

Singapore has many schemes for lower-income households, seniors, workers and families. This page points you to the main ones. It does not list amounts or income limits, because these change.

## Everyday cost-of-living help

- **GST Voucher:** support for eligible lower- to middle-income citizens to offset GST costs. Check eligibility and how payment is made on the official site.
- **Community Development Council (CDC) Vouchers:** vouchers for households, typically to spend at participating heartland merchants and hawkers.
- **Utilities and other rebates:** announced at the Budget, so check the latest announcements.

## Help for seniors

- **Silver Support Scheme:** regular cash support for eligible seniors with lower lifetime income.
- **Pioneer Generation and Merdeka Generation Packages:** healthcare and other benefits for the respective cohorts.
- **Retirement adequacy:** see [CPF](money-cpf.html) for CPF LIFE and top-ups.

## Help for workers

- **Workfare Income Supplement (WIS) and Workfare Skills Support:** support for eligible lower-wage workers, paid partly into CPF.
- **SkillsFuture** and job-matching support for those between jobs (see [Employment](employment.html) and [Education](education-skills.html)).

## Help in difficult times

- **ComCare:** financial and social assistance for households facing hardship, applied for through a Social Service Office.
- **Healthcare support:** MediFund, CHAS and subsidies (see [Healthcare](healthcare.html)).
- **Housing support:** HDB assistance for those in need (see [Housing](housing.html)).

## How to find what you qualify for

- Use **SupportGoWhere** to find schemes by your situation.
- Visit a **Social Service Office (SSO)** for personal help.
- Do not pay agents to apply on your behalf. Applications are free.
