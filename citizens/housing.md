# Housing

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Confirm all details on the official sites._

Most Singapore citizens live in public housing built and managed by the Housing & Development Board (HDB).

## Ways to buy

- **Build-To-Order (BTO):** new flats launched in sales exercises, applied for online.
- **Resale flats:** buy from another owner on the open market.
- **Sale of Balance Flats / Open-Booking:** remaining flats from earlier launches.

## Things to check

- **Eligibility:** citizenship, age, family nucleus, income ceilings and ownership rules differ by scheme and flat type.
- **Grants:** schemes such as the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant may help eligible buyers. Amounts and criteria change.
- **Financing:** HDB loan versus bank loan, and using CPF OA for the down payment and instalments.
- **Minimum Occupation Period (MOP):** the period you must live in the flat before selling or renting it out wholly.
- **Home Protection Scheme:** mortgage insurance for HDB loans, paid through CPF.
