# Incentives, Credits, Rebates & Top-ups

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Schemes, amounts and eligibility change, often at the annual Budget. This page lists what exists and where to check. It does not give amounts or income limits._

## Credits and vouchers

- **SkillsFuture Credit:** for eligible citizens to use on approved courses. Additional credit may be given to specific age groups or mid-career workers.
- **CDC Vouchers and GST Voucher:** announced at the Budget. Claim through the official site and never through links sent in messages.
- **Child Development Account:** the Government matches parents' deposits up to a limit.

## Tax reliefs and rebates

- **Reliefs** reduce your taxable income. The common ones are:
  - CPF cash top-up relief
  - SRS contributions
  - Parent relief
  - Child relief
  - Course fees relief
- **Rebates** reduce the tax you owe, and may be announced for a specific year.
- Claim reliefs when you file, or check that IRAS has pre-filled them. Keep your supporting documents.
- More at [Tax (IRAS)](tax.html).

## CPF top-ups and bonuses

- **Voluntary cash top-ups** to your own or a family member's Special/Retirement Account or MediSave can earn tax relief.
- **Government top-ups** appear in specific years for eligible members, and are credited automatically.
- The **Matched Retirement Savings Scheme** matches cash top-ups for eligible seniors, up to a limit.
- More at [Money & retirement (CPF)](money-cpf.html).

## Housing and family

- **Housing grants** apply to eligible buyers and depend on factors such as income, flat type and household. See [Housing](housing.html).
- **Baby Bonus, CDA and childcare subsidies** support families with young children. See [Family & life events](family-life-events.html).

## Work and training

- **Workfare** and **SkillsFuture** schemes, plus training allowances for those between jobs. See [Employment](employment.html) and [Education & skills](education-skills.html).

## Tips

- Check **how you will be paid** (cash, CPF, MediSave, vouchers). It is not always the same.
- **Never click payment links** about credits or rebates in SMS or WhatsApp. Government agencies tell you to log in through the official site or app. See [Safety & scams](safety-scams.html).
- Eligibility often depends on your **Assessable Income, Annual Value of your home and number of properties**. Check the current criteria.
- Unclaimed credits can expire, so note expiry dates.
