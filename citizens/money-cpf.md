# Money & Retirement (CPF)

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Confirm all figures on the official sites._

The Central Provident Fund (CPF) is Singapore's mandatory savings scheme for citizens and Permanent Residents, covering retirement, healthcare and housing.

## Your CPF accounts

- **Ordinary Account (OA):** housing, insurance, investment and education.
- **Special Account (SA):** retirement savings and investment (closed at age 55 and merged into the Retirement Account).
- **MediSave Account (MA):** hospitalisation, approved outpatient treatments and health insurance premiums.
- **Retirement Account (RA):** created at age 55 to provide monthly payouts in retirement.

## Key things to know

- Contribution rates depend on your age and wage, and are set by the CPF Board. Check the current table rather than relying on older figures.
- **CPF LIFE** is the national annuity scheme that gives lifelong monthly payouts.
- You can top up your own or your family members' accounts, and may get tax relief for doing so.
- **Supplementary Retirement Scheme (SRS)** is a voluntary scheme that offers tax benefits on contributions.
- Nominate your CPF beneficiaries so your savings go where you intend.
