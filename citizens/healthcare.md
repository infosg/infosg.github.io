# Healthcare

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Confirm all details on the official sites._

Singapore's system is built on "3M": MediSave, MediShield Life and MediFund, with government subsidies for public healthcare.

## Key schemes

- **MediShield Life:** basic health insurance for all citizens and PRs, covering large hospital bills for life. Premiums can be paid with MediSave, with premium subsidies for lower-income households.
- **Integrated Shield Plans:** optional private insurance that adds to MediShield Life for higher ward classes.
- **MediSave:** use your CPF MediSave for hospitalisation and approved treatments.
- **MediFund:** safety net for those who cannot afford their bills after other help.
- **CareShield Life:** long-term care insurance for severe disability.
- **Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS):** subsidies for GP and dental care at participating clinics.
- **Healthier SG:** enrol with a family doctor of your choice to focus on preventive care.

## Useful tools

- **HealthHub** for appointments, medical records, vaccination records and bills.
- **Organ donation (HOTA):** under the Human Organ Transplant Act, citizens and PRs are included by default unless they opt out.
