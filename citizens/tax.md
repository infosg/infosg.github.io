# Tax (IRAS)

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Tax rates, reliefs and deadlines change often. Confirm all figures on IRAS._

Income tax in Singapore is administered by the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS). Singapore tax residents are taxed on income earned here, at progressive rates.

## Filing your income tax

- Many taxpayers are covered by **auto-inclusion**, where employers send income data to IRAS and you do not need to file unless notified.
- If IRAS sends a notice to file, do so by the stated deadline through **myTax Portal** using your Singpass.
- Keep your records (receipts, relief claims) for the period IRAS requires.

## Reliefs and rebates

- Reliefs reduce your taxable income. Common ones relate to **CPF top-ups, SRS contributions, parent and child relief, course fees** and others.
- Eligibility conditions and caps change. Use the IRAS list of reliefs, not older figures.

## Paying your tax

- Your **Notice of Assessment** shows the amount due and the payment deadline.
- Payment options include GIRO, online banking and PayNow. Instalment plans through GIRO are available.
- Pay on time. Late payment can attract penalties.

## Property tax and GST

- Owner-occupiers and landlords pay **property tax** based on annual value. IRAS issues the bill.
- **GST** is charged on most goods and services. Some households receive GST Voucher support (see [Support schemes](support-schemes.html)).

## Beware of tax scams

IRAS will not ask you to share your Singpass, bank login or OTP, or to transfer money to resolve a tax issue. When in doubt, call IRAS using the number on its official website.

## Official sources

- IRAS: <https://www.iras.gov.sg>
- myTax Portal (via IRAS)
- CPF Board (top-ups and relief): <https://www.cpf.gov.sg>
