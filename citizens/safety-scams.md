## Protect yourself from scams

- Government officers and banks will **never** ask you to transfer money or share OTPs or passwords over a phone call, SMS or messaging app.
- Do not click links in unexpected messages. Type the official website address yourself.
- Install the **ScamShield** app to filter scam calls and SMSes.
- Check suspicious offers or numbers with the **ScamShield helpline (1799)** or the **Anti-Scam Helpline**.
- If you think you've been scammed, call your bank immediately to freeze transactions, then report to the police.
- Turn on two-factor authentication on your Singpass, banking and email accounts.
