# Family & Life Events

_Last reviewed: November 2026. Confirm all details on the official sites._

## Getting married

- Marriages are registered with the **Registry of Marriages (ROM)**. Civil marriages require a notice of marriage and meeting the legal requirements, and some religious ceremonies have their own registration arrangements.
- Update your records (such as marital status) after marriage.

## Having a baby

- Register your child's **birth** through the official birth registration process. Births in hospitals are generally registered with the hospital's help, but parents still need to complete the steps.
- Look up the **Baby Bonus** scheme and other child-related support on MSF.
- Enrol the child in **MediSave and MediShield Life** coverage (see [Healthcare](healthcare.html)).
- For infant and childcare, check the **Early Childhood Development Agency (ECDA)** for subsidies and preschool options.

## When someone dies

- A **doctor or hospital** issues the cause-of-death certification. The death must be registered and a death certificate obtained.
- Notify relevant organisations: CPF, banks, insurers, IRAS and housing agencies.
- Look at CPF nominations and any will. Estates without a will are distributed under the law.

## Planning ahead

- **Will:** states how your assets are distributed. Consider writing one with legal advice.
- **Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA):** lets you appoint someone to make decisions for you if you lose mental capacity. Apply through the Office of the Public Guardian.
- **Advance Care Planning:** record your wishes for future medical care.
- **CPF nominations:** these are separate from a will, so keep them current.
